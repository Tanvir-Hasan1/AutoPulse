Design Philosophy Motocross & Telemetry

The AutoPulse brand identity relies on vehicle telemetry tracking. By analyzing your motocross ride image (bike.png), we extracted the high-energy athletic jump stance of the rider and bike wheels.

We vectorized this geometry and overlaid a flowing electric diagnostic pulse running directly through the peak of the motocross chassis, symbolizing that the "Pulse" represents real-time performance and system checks under heavy load.
